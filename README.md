# CIT423003-MDL

**Multimodales Deep Learning (CIT423003) / Multimodal Deep Learning (CIT423003)**

This repository contains lecture materials and my notes from *Multimodal Deep Learning (CIT423003)*, which I attended as a excahnge student (TUMexchange program) at TUM during the Summer Semester 2025.

The course was organized by the **Chair for AI in Healthcare and Medicine (AIM Lab)**, under the direction of Prof. Daniel Rückert. The teaching team included Group Leader Dr. Martin Menten and the PhD students Philip Müller, Florian Hölzl, and David Mildenberger.
